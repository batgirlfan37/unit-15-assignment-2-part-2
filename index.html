WELCOME TO THE SWAFFHAM SWIFTS

the up and coming esports team

Few teams make it in the esports scene. Even fewer win. Even fewer still do it as quickly as the swaffham swifts. Consisting of 5 like minded individuals hailing from swaffham, the masterclass team of Denji, Makima, Aki, Himeno and Power work together to obliterate the competition with ease. From first person shooters like Overwatch and Call of Duty to fighting games like Mortal Kombat and Tekken, the team has shown their dedication, skill and resilience, already beating some of the other big teams from the region such as the Cromer Crabs, Dersingham Dynamos and Lynn Lions. This team not only SHOWS potential, but knocks it out of the water
