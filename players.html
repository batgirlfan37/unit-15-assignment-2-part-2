DENJI

DAMAGE

Denji is the human hybrid between man and chainsaw devil. bonded in death with the chainsaw devil, in the form of Pochita. His death caused pochita to give Denji his heart in exchange for a chance to see Denji's dreams realised. This led to Denji gaining the powers of the Chainsaw Devil, and is able to pull the chord on his heart to activate his powers, which he uses to hunt dangerous devils as part of the devil hunting unit, Public Safety.

AKI

TANK

Meme Bashame is our team's laid back support, both in games and out of them. Bashame is an established rice farmer and entreprenuer, keeping the team fed and healthy whenever she isnt keeping them alive in game. Bashame has an impressive history at her previous location, the Deer Club at Hinominami High School, Japan. At the Deer Club, she served as a rookie deer to her senpai, Nokotan, where she learnt dedication, skills and deer related expertise alongside her fellow deer club members. Her rice making expertise carried them back then, and continue to going forward under her newest endeavour, the Swaffham Swifts. Bashame has mentioned how she hopes to raise enough money to one day retire and create a country spanning rice paddy, and so is dedicated until then, to make her dreams come true.
